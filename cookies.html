Cookie Policy

A cookie is a small file stored on your device when you visit a website. This policy explains what cookies, if any, are used on virginia-legacy.pages.dev.

1. What we use

We do not set marketing, advertising, or analytics cookies. We do not run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any ad tracker on this site as of the date above.

Our service providers may set limited technical cookies to deliver the site:

  • Cloudflare Pages, our host. Cloudflare may set security and load-balancing cookies to protect the site and deliver pages reliably.
  • Google Forms, when you sign up. Submitting the mailing list form loads Google Forms. Google may set cookies needed for that form to work.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr. These load fonts and a script for the site. They do not set a cookie for Virginia Legacy, but your browser may send your IP address to them when loading those files.

2. What we do not do

We do not use cookies to track you across other websites, to build an advertising profile, or to sell your browsing data.

3. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking technical cookies may make parts of the site, including the mailing list form, stop working. No cookie consent banner is shown on this site today because no non-essential cookies are set by us.

4. Changes

If we add analytics, advertising, a shop checkout, or any non-essential cookie, we will update this policy first and add a consent banner before that cookie is set.

5. Contact

Cookie questions: info@radioplayinc.com. Also see our Privacy Policy.
