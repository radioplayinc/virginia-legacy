Accessibility Statement

Virginia Legacy wants this site to be usable by everyone, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, or other assistive technology.

1. Our standard

Our goal is to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. That is the standard most US businesses use for ADA compliance. We are not there yet on every page. The known gaps are listed below, honestly, so you know what we are fixing.

2. What works today

  • The site uses real headings, labels, and buttons, not just styled text.
  • Most photos have descriptive alt text.
  • The email field has a label and a visible focus outline, and form errors are announced.
  • A skip link has been added at the top of the home page, so keyboard users can jump to the main content.
  • The page title now changes from "Loading" to "Virginia Legacy" once the site loads.

3. Known issues we are fixing

  • Intro video has no captions or transcript yet. The entry film autoplays and can be skipped after about 5 seconds. A transcript and captions are on the fix list before the next public push.
  • Contrast needs a measured check. White text over the court photo background and white text on orange buttons has not been measured against WCAG contrast ratios yet. We will test and darken text or backgrounds where it fails.
  • Single-page navigation. Team, Coaches, Tickets, and Shop are views inside one page, with no separate URLs. That makes it harder to share or return to a section, and harder for some assistive tools to track where you are. We will review this when Shop and Tickets open.
  • Some images have empty alt text. A few decorative images are correctly empty. Any image that carries meaning should have alt text, and we will audit each one.

4. Feedback and help

If you cannot use part of this site, tell us what page, what device and browser you used, and what happened. We will try to provide the information another way, for example by email or phone, while we fix the page.

Contact: info@radioplayinc.com.

5. Ongoing work

We will re-check accessibility each time we add a section, product, or video, and update this statement when a known issue is fixed.
