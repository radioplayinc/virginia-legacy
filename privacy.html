Privacy Policy

Virginia Legacy Women's Basketball ("Virginia Legacy", "we", "us") operates virginia-legacy.pages.dev. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what choices you have. It covers only this website. It does not cover Google, Cloudflare, or any other site linked from this site.

Contact: Privacy questions and deletion requests go to info@radioplayinc.com.

1. What we collect

Email address, only when you give it to us. The "Join our mailing list" form asks for one thing: your email address. We do not ask for your name, address, phone number, payment details, or date of birth on this site.

Signup time. When you join, Google Forms records the time of your signup along with your email address.

Technical information from our service providers. This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts and a script from jsDelivr. Those providers may receive your IP address and browser information when your browser loads the site, as is normal for any website. We do not run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any advertising tracker on this site as of the date above.

We do not collect precise location, health information, or financial information.

2. How we use your email

We use your email address only to send Legacy news, ticket drops, and shop releases, as stated on the signup form. We do not use it for any other purpose.

We do not sell your email address. We do not share it for advertising. We do not rent our mailing list.

3. Who receives your information

  • Google Forms and Google Sheets. Your signup is submitted to a Google Form called "Virginia Legacy Mailing List" and stored in the linked Google Sheet of the same name. Google processes that information on our behalf under Google's terms.
  • Cloudflare. Cloudflare hosts this site and may process technical logs to deliver and protect it.
  • Legal requirements. We will disclose information only if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of Virginia Legacy and its visitors.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Unsubscribed addresses are removed from future mailings.

5. Your choices and rights

Every marketing email we send will include an unsubscribe link. You can also:

  • Ask what email address we hold for you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Ask us to stop emailing you.

To make any of those requests, email info@radioplayinc.com from the address you signed up with and tell us what you want changed or deleted. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Residents of California and other states with privacy laws may have additional rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by those laws. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

6. Cookies

We do not set marketing or advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy for the limited technical cookies our hosting and font providers may set.

7. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The mailing list is for people 13 and older. If you are a parent and believe your child under 13 signed up, email info@radioplayinc.com and we will delete that address.

8. Security

This site uses HTTPS. Access to the mailing list sheet is limited to the Virginia Legacy team. No system is completely secure, so do not send sensitive information through the mailing list form. It is for an email address only.

9. Changes to this policy

If we add analytics, advertising, a shop checkout, ticket sales, or any new way of collecting information, we will update this policy before that change goes live, and update the date at the top.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: info@radioplayinc.com.
