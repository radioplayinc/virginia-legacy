Coaches — Spotlight

Tamecka Dixon

Head Coach • Kansas ’97 • WNBA 1997–2009

A guard who helped launch the WNBA in its very first season, Dixon won back-to-back titles with the Los Angeles Sparks, earned three straight All-Star selections, and wore USA gold — then came home to coach, taking over the girls’ program at Linden High School, the school where her own jersey is retired.

2× WNBA Champion Sparks · 2001 & 2002
3× WNBA All-Star 2001 · 2002 · 2003
13 WNBA Seasons 360 games · 9.7 PPG
Gold Team USA 2002 Worlds · China
